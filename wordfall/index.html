Wordfall

A word game where the letters fall. Spell words, clear the board, and plan the cascade.

Support

Questions, a bug, or a word you think should count? Email riley.brodericka@gmail.com. Include your device and iOS version if something isn't working.

Restoring purchases

Remove Ads and season passes come back with Settings › More › Restore Purchases, using the same Apple Account you bought them with. Coins from coin packs are kept with your progress in iCloud.

Moving to a new device

Your progress, stars and coins sync through iCloud. Sign in to the same Apple Account with iCloud on, open Wordfall, and give it a moment to catch up.

Refunds

Purchases are made through Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
